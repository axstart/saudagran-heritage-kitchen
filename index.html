Heritage engine

A 60-year story,
told in three chapters

Allahabad → Pakistan → Saudi Arabia → Malaysia

Merchant-family spice trade in the Allahabad bazaars: whole cardamom, clove, and saffron bought by the sack, ground at home, and cooked into the first sealed pots the household still remembers.

Migration carried the masala tin to Pakistan, then to Saudi Arabia — where Arab hospitality and South Asian heat met. Dum cooking slowed further; pickles matured longer; the family palate became a fusion, never a compromise.

The kitchen now lives in Kuala Lumpur. Nanny directs every pot — Heritage Dum Biryani, Royal Shahi Dahi Baray, and the newly launched Mirch Ka Achar — for families across Greater Klang Valley who want the taste of a merchant house, not a food court.
